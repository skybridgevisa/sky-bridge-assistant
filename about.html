About Sky Bridge

Guidance for your international plans.

Sky Bridge provides visa consultancy and application assistance for people exploring international education, travel, work and related opportunities.

Our approach

Professional, clear and practical.

We help clients understand general visa requirements, organize documentation and navigate the application process with clear communication.

Because visa rules differ by destination and applicant circumstances, personalized cases are directed to a counselor rather than treated with one-size-fits-all answers.
