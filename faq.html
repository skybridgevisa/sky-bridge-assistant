Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions.

Visa requirements and processing times vary by destination and applicant circumstances.

What documents are generally required for a study visa?

Common categories can include a valid passport, photographs, academic records, financial evidence and supporting institution documents. Exact requirements depend on the destination and institution.

How does the Sky Bridge consultation process work?

You can submit the consultation form or contact the counselor. We can discuss your destination and visa category, review general documentation categories and explain application-assistance options.

Can Sky Bridge assist with work permits?

Yes. Sky Bridge provides guidance and application assistance related to work permits. Requirements vary by country and job situation.

Can Sky Bridge assist with tourist visas?

Yes. Sky Bridge can provide general documentation and application guidance for tourist/visit visa cases, subject to destination-specific requirements.

How long does visa processing take?

Processing times vary by destination, visa category, application volume and individual circumstances. The relevant immigration authority makes the final decision.

What happens if a visa application is refused?

The next steps depend on the refusal reason and destination rules. A counselor can help you understand available application-assistance options.

Can I apply without using a consultant?

Where the relevant authority permits self-application, applicants may apply directly. Sky Bridge's consultancy is an optional assistance service and does not replace official authority requirements.
